Cut, pin, stitch

Felt appliqué.

Ten shapes at true size for decorating a wool felt hat, a bag or a sauna gift: birch leaves, spruce, snowflake, star, heart, flame, steam, ladle, drop and a name patch.

  1. Print at 100%Check the squares on the template pages: 1 in and 2 cm exactly.
  2. Cut the paper templateCut on the solid line. For small or pointy shapes, glue the page to thin card first.
  3. Trace and cut the feltHold the template on the felt and draw around it, or pin the paper on and cut through both.
  4. Pin it on the hatTry a few spots. Pin or clip the piece in place, away from any seam.
  5. Sew around the edgeBlanket stitch or whip stitch along the dashed line. Keep stitches shallow in the hat felt.
  6. Add detailsVeins, stars or a letter in back stitch or stem stitch on top of the piece.

What you need

  • Felt:wool or wool-blend craft felt, about 1–2 mm thick. It cuts cleanly and does not fray.
  • Thread:cotton embroidery floss (2 or 3 strands) or pearl cotton, matching or contrasting.
  • Needle:sharp embroidery or chenille needle, plus a thimble.
  • Also:small sharp scissors, pins or clips, a pencil or chalk pencil.
Blanket stitchNeat ladder edge. Needle in through the piece, out at the edge, over the thread loop.
Whip stitchFast and almost invisible in matching thread. Small slanted stitches over the edge.
Running stitchA dotted line just inside the edge. Also good for leaf veins and steam.
Sew, don’t glue

Hot glue and iron-on adhesive can soften in sauna heat, and ironing means heat and steam on the hat. A few stitches hold for years.

Keep the inside smooth

Hat felt is about 4–5 mm thick. Take shallow stitches through the outer part where you can, and keep knots small and flat.

No metal

Skip metal charms, sequins and metallic thread. Metal gets hot in the sauna.

Ideas

Drawings are not to scale. Start with one piece; you can always add another.

Mix two colors

Light pieces on dark hats, dark on light. The flame and its center look best in two warm colors.

Mark your hat

In a busy sauna a leaf or an initial makes your hat easy to find on the peg.

Small at the edge

Keep pieces clear of the bottom edge if you like to fold it up, so the fold stays soft.

Wear it dry

A sauna hat is worn dry. If it ever needs a wash: by hand only, in cool, room-temperature water (about 68–75 °F / 20–24 °C, never warm or hot), squeeze, don’t wring, air-dry.

No machine, no dryer

Never machine-wash or tumble-dry a felt hat, decorated or not. Brush off lint with a dry clothes brush.

Loose stitch?

Fix it right away with a few stitches in matching thread, before the piece starts to lift.
